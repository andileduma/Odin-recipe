Shredded Zucchini Casserole

Nutrition Facts

Per Serving: 250 calories | 12g fat | 20g carbohydrates | 15g protein

Unleash the flavors of summer with our Shredded Zucchini Casserole recipe!

This dish is a delightful combination of fresh zucchini, savory cheese, and a hint of herbs, baked to perfection.

Perfect as a side dish or a light main course, this casserole is sure to become a family favorite.

Follow our step-by-step instructions to create this delicious and comforting meal that celebrates the best of seasonal produce.

Ingredients

  • 8 cups coarsely shredded zucchini
  • 1/2 teaspoon salt
  • 1 tablespoon olive oil
  • 1 cup finely chopped onion
  • 1/4 cup chopped fresh parsley
  • 4 cloves garlic, minced
  • 4 large eggs
  • 1 1/2 cups shredded mozzarella cheese
  • 1/2 teaspoon freshly ground black pepper
  • 1 1/2 cups shredded sharp Cheddar cheese
  • 3/4 cup grated Parmesan cheese, divided
  • 2/3 cup Italian seasoned panko breadcrumbs
  • 3 tablespoons butter, melted
  • chopped chives to taste, for garnish

Directions

Step 1

Gather all ingredients. Preheat the oven to 375 degrees F (190 degrees C).

Lightly grease a 2-quart rectangular baking dish.

Step 2

Combine zucchini and salt in a large bowl and toss to coat evenly.

Transfer zucchini to a colander set in the sink. Let stand to drain excess moisture, 15 minutes.

Transfer zucchini to a clean kitchen towel and squeeze out any remaining liquid.

Use paper towels to wipe the large bowl dry.

Step 3

Heat oil in a large skillet over medium heat.

Add onion and cook until soft and translucent, about 4 minutes.

Add garlic and cook until fragrant, 1 minute more.

Step 4

Whisk together eggs and pepper in the large bowl.

Add zucchini, onion mixture, mozzarella, Cheddar,

and 1/2 cup of the Parmesan cheese; combine thoroughly.

Step 5

Transfer mixture to the prepared baking dish and spread into an even layer.

Step 6

Bake, uncovered, for 20 minutes.

Step 7

Meanwhile, combine panko and melted butter in a small bowl.

Stir in remaining 1/4 cup Parmesan cheese.

Step 8

Top casserole with breadcrumb mixture and bake until golden and crisp, 10 to 15 minutes.

Garnish with chives.
